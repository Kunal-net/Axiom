1. Unilateral termination without notice

High Risk
Section 5.2 • Page 4
Contract Excerpt

“The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, for any reason, without prior notice and without liability.”

report_problem Why this poses high risk
  • No guaranteed cure or notice period (industry norm: 14–30 days).
  • Eliminates compensation rights for work initiated or currently in progress.
  • Completely non-reciprocal: you remain bound to deliverables while client can vacate commitments instantaneously.
auto_fix_high Recommended Revision

“Either party may terminate this Agreement with 30 days’ written notice. The Client shall compensate the Freelancer for all work completed up to the termination date.”

2. Broad intellectual property ownership

High Risk
Section 8.1 • Page 5
Contract Excerpt

“Client claims full ownership of all work produced including pre-existing developer tooling, proprietary libraries, and background know-how.”

report_problem Why this poses high risk
  • May strip your legal ability to reuse your own personal frameworks, scripts, or boilerplates with future clients.
  • Substantially exceeds traditional statutory “Work for Hire” boundaries by annexing pre-existing background IP.
auto_fix_high Recommended Revision

“Client owns all newly created custom deliverables upon final payment. Freelancer retains all rights to pre-existing tools, libraries, and frameworks, granting Client a perpetual, royalty-free license to use them solely within the deliverable.”

3. Unlimited indemnity and liability

Medium Risk
Section 11 • Page 6
Contract Excerpt

“Freelancer agrees to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Client against any and all claims, damages, liabilities, costs, and expenses arising out of the performance of services.”

report_problem Why this poses risk
  • Uncapped financial exposure that exceeds the total monetary value of the contract.
  • Includes consequential and indirect losses beyond direct tangible damages.
  • Lacks reciprocity: Client does not indemnify Freelancer for Client-provided assets.
auto_fix_high Recommended Revision

“Freelancer liability shall be capped at the total fees paid under this Agreement. Indemnification applies solely to direct third-party intellectual property infringement claims resulting directly from Freelancer deliverables.”

4. Governing law & exclusive jurisdiction

Low Risk
Section 14 • Page 8
Contract Excerpt

“This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, and any disputes shall be resolved in Wilmington courts.”

info Analytical Assessment

Delaware corporate venue is standard for US enterprise contracts, but may incur remote litigation expenses if you reside outside the state. While standard, mutual mediation could be inserted prior to formal court actions.
